# Surface brief — Standings (LeaderboardsScreen), Arena Plates pass

**Mode:** Operate (the visitor reads a ranking and resolves a reward
notice; scanability and state clarity outrank expression).

**Job:** Standings is the Hunter tab's competitive record: the
settled weekly-earnings record (the Standings door badge's
destination — DESIGN.md §3/§9), the current week's races, last
week's finals, and the global board across 10 metrics × 3 periods.

**Direction (owner-pinned):** Arena Plates — the boss-arena plate
register approved for the Hunter menu (anchor:
`hunter-menu-captures-2/look-plates/01-first-screen.png`). This
surface's own composition inside that family: **the standings
bracket**. The board is ONE machined plate, not a card of rows:
rank is the hero numeral in a milled well, the top three form a
podium head, ordinary entries are bracket rungs separated by steel
hairlines inside the plate, and the player's own row — wherever it
sits — is a bolted-on plate of its own so it can never be lost in
the list. Race boards are heat plates; the earnings record is a
pay plate stamped PAID BY SETTLEMENT (race rewards auto-pay;
there is no claim — DESIGN.md §3).

**Laws carried in:** no colored side bars (color lives in
numerals, chips, foot strips, light); PxIcon only; 11px type
floor; tap targets ≥44px; numbers never truncate; amber =
action waiting only (settled money prints in amber-glow/key
yellow, the game's cash color); real data shapes only — trend is
value-vs-prior from the real payload, never an invented rank delta.

**Keep every real function:** earnings panel states
(earnings/pending/placed/empty + seen-marking + focus landing),
4 races/week current + previous finals with my-reward lines and
the drop-luck buff, metric picker (10 metrics), period switch
(3), row tap → hunter profile, my-row pinning outside the shown
entries.
