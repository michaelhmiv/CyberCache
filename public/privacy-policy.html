CyberCache Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CyberCache collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the CyberCache mobile app, web app, testing builds, and related services.

Questions or data requests can be sent to michaelhmiv@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

CyberCache may collect the following information:

  • Account information, such as email address, account identifiers, display name, authentication provider identifiers, and guest account state.
  • Gameplay and progression data, such as profile state, inventory, cache keys, loadouts, activity, crafting, boss progress, marketplace activity, rewards, settings, and related game records.
  • Device and notification data, such as Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens and app platform details when you enable push notifications.
  • Advertising and rewarded ad data from Google AdMob, such as ad interactions, device identifiers, approximate location signals, and diagnostics processed by Google to serve and measure ads.
  • Technical data, such as IP address, user agent, request logs, server diagnostics, app version, crash or error information, and security logs created by hosting and service providers.
  • Local app data stored on your device, such as session persistence, runtime display state, and app preferences.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Operate accounts, authentication, and saved game progress.
  • Provide gameplay features, rewards, inventory, and app settings.
  • Verify server-authoritative game actions and prevent abuse.
  • Send optional push notifications related to app activity.
  • Show rewarded ads and credit eligible in-game rewards after completion.
  • Maintain app security, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability.
  • Distribute tester builds and install links during pre-release testing.
  • Comply with legal, platform, and safety requirements.

Rewarded Ads

CyberCache uses Google AdMob to provide rewarded video ads. When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google may process information such as device identifiers, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and approximate location signals under Google's policies. CyberCache grants the in-game reward only after the ad flow reports a completed reward event and the server verifies the reward claim.

Learn more from the Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob privacy information.

Push Notifications

If you allow notifications, CyberCache may register a device token through Firebase Cloud Messaging so the app can send relevant gameplay or account notifications. You can disable notifications through your device settings.

Service Providers

CyberCache uses service providers to run the app and testing workflows, including Supabase for authentication and database services, Railway for hosting, Google Firebase for push notifications and tester distribution, Google AdMob for ads, Google Play for Android distribution, and GitHub Actions for build automation. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services, comply with law, protect security, and operate their platforms.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers listed above, when required by law, to protect users or the app, or as part of a business transfer involving CyberCache.

Data Retention

Account and gameplay data is retained while your account remains active or as needed to provide the app, resolve disputes, maintain security, and comply with legal or platform obligations. Logs and diagnostics are retained for limited operational periods unless needed for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your CyberCache account and associated server-side gameplay data by emailing michaelhmiv@gmail.com. Please include the email address or account identifier used for CyberCache. Device-only data can also be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

See the CyberCache Data Deletion Instructions for the dedicated deletion request page.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including provider-managed authentication, access controls, server-side verification for progression changes, and encrypted transport where supported. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

CyberCache is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

International Processing

Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where CyberCache and its service providers operate. Privacy protections may differ from those in your location.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.

Contact

CyberCache privacy and support requests: michaelhmiv@gmail.com
