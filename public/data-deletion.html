CyberCache Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: June 14, 2026

CyberCache users can request deletion of their account and associated server-side gameplay data by contacting support.

Email: michaelhmiv@gmail.com

How To Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to michaelhmiv@gmail.com with the subject line "CyberCache data deletion request".
  2. Include the email address, username, or account identifier used for CyberCache so we can locate the account.
  3. We may ask for confirmation before deleting server-side account data.

Data Deleted

A deletion request is intended to delete account records and associated server-side gameplay data, including profile state, inventory, gameplay progression, cache key balances, push notification registration, and related account records where deletion is technically and legally available.

Data That May Be Retained

Some records may be retained for limited periods when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, financial or platform reporting, or backup integrity. Provider logs may remain according to each provider's retention policies.

Device-Only Data

Data stored only on your device can be removed by clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling CyberCache.
