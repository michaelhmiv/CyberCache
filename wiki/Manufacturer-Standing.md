# Manufacturer Standing

Each of the six weapon manufacturers keeps an **independent standing ladder** — reputation earned per maker, with no opposed or negative standing: raising one never lowers another. Standing points come from turning in that maker's gear and from owning it; the balance is never spent, and ranks unlock only when you pay the rank-up **tribute** (a full ladder costs about 410,000 credits plus 3 items across its ranks).

Each ladder pays out five unlocks: a Rep Shelf storefront, an exclusive recipe, a rank-3 cosmetic, a weekly Parts Crate, and a weekly Signature Order — a Rare-to-Epic piece from that maker, never Legendary. Note that turning in a Legendary earns standing only: it mints **no** legendary core (cores come from salvage; see [[Collections]]).
