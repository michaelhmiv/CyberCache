# Activity Log

The Activity Log is your hunter's whole record — every drop, trade, craft, and fight, kept from your first day, newest first. Nothing is ever cut off: scroll back far enough and you reach the start of the account.

**Two doors lead here.** On [[Home|Home]], the loot feed's foot carries **See full log** — the feed shows the last few facts; the log keeps all of them. On the Hunter tab, the **Profile** stop carries an **Activity Log** door directly under the Lifetime Record panel: the totals say what you have, the log says how it happened.

## What lands in the log

- **Drops**, each with its receipt (below)
- **Kill stretches** — roaming kills land as one summary line per stretch, not one line per kill; the drops they produced are their own entries
- **[[Bosses|Boss fights]]** — outcome, the win chance going in, credits won or lost, and the victory loot
- **[[Caches]]** — the item, the outcome, and the draw exactly as rolled
- **[[Crafting|Crafts]]** — recipe, units, bench, XP, and what came off the bench
- **[[The Market|Market trades]]** — price, fee, and who was on the other side; for your own sales, the exact amount you were credited (price minus fee, plus any proceeds bonus) — the figure the Sold announcement on Home shows
- **Earnings claims** — credits, scrap, XP, fragments, materials, and essences, with the amounts up front
- **[[Dispatch]] settlements, [[Endless Mode|Endless]] runs, salvage payouts, and system events** like resurrections

## Receipts and odds

Every drop since the receipts update is **stamped at the moment it drops** — where it came from, and the odds it rolled. Tap any entry in the log to open its record:

- **Where it came from** — the source (roaming, a boss, a cache, the Endless, while-away settling), the area, and the enemy involved, in honest words. A drop that *rolled while you were fighting* an enemy says exactly that; **"dropped by"** is printed only when the kill itself caused the drop — a boss, an elite, a bounty, an alpha.
- **The odds it rolled** — the headline chance plus every factor that shaped it: the tick's drop chance, your luck in force, any rarity floor, boost, or dedicated boss chance. These numbers are recorded on the item and never change after the fact — selling or salvaging the item doesn't erase its story.

**Older drops predate item records.** They still appear in the log with their name, rarity, and time — the log just can't source them, and says so instead of guessing. A **Receipts begin here** line marks the boundary in the stream.

**Cache draws are shown as rolled** — the roll, the table's total weight, and your cache quality. Quality shapes the rarity weights before the roll, so the log shows the draw itself rather than a percentage it can't prove after the fact.

## While you were away

Offline progress rides the same stream. A **While you were away** divider opens each away stretch: the settlement entry (time away, ticks settled, whether the offline cap was reached), then the kills, earnings, and drops the settler produced — each drop with its own receipt, marked as away-earned.

## Finding things

- **One search box** covers item names, enemy names, and area names together — search "Vulture" and you get every drop that names one, from either relation.
- **Kind chips** filter the stream: Loot, Kills, Boss, Caches, Craft, Market, Earnings, Away, Salvage, Dispatch, Endless, System.
- **Load older facts** walks the record back page by page; the end of the stream names the date it reaches back to.

Guests see their own log in full — the log records guests exactly as it records linked accounts, and nothing in it is a transaction.
