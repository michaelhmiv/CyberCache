# Vehicles & the Garage

Vehicles are real gear: item instances in their own slot, earned and equipped like anything else. A vehicle multiplies your roaming **yield** — it does not change the 3-second tick rate — with each class trading yield against level reach: the Scrap Bike (×1.15, your starter) up through the Dune Buggy, Utility Rover, Cargo Hauler, and Armored Crawler, the high-yield Relay Skimmer, and the level-75 Leviathan Rig (×1.50).

Vehicles burn **crafted fuel cells**; run dry and the bonus simply goes inactive — roaming continues on foot, never blocked. Condition decays with use and degrades the bonus but never destroys the vehicle, and **patch kits** (also crafted) restore it — yourself, slowly and untrained, or through a [[Repair Board]] professional. Your Garage lives inside the Gear tab's Loadout view.
