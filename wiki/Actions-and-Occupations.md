# Actions & Occupations

Your hunter has **one personal action at a time** — roam *or* craft *or* extract — plus boss fights and endless runs, which also use that slot. Two things run in parallel by design: **Dispatch** (send spare loadouts on missions) and **Research** (the education track, a deliberate exception for schooling only).

- **Roaming** is the core idle activity: 3-second ticks in one of 7 areas, paying credits, XP, scrap, and loot. Pacing, offline earnings, and the 24-hour absolute offline cap are covered in [[Progression]].
- **Crafting** turns materials into gear and consumables through timed workshop jobs — only while crafting is your active action — and pays XP at about 77% of roaming's rate. See [[Crafting]].
- **Extraction** runs 10-minute passes into the wilds for materials and signature finds, paying XP at about 75% of roaming's rate.
- **Research** enrolls you in courses (12 in the catalog) that run on wall-clock time — even offline, even while you do something else — unlocking batch recipes and granting mastery instead of XP.
- **Dispatch & courier** missions are picked from a date-seeded board; courier tiers gate on vehicle class, and extra queue slots are capped (2 by default).
