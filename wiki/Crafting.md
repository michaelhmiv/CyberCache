# Crafting

Crafting is an occupation, not a menu: workshop jobs advance **only while crafting is your active action** (see [[Actions and Occupations]]), and they pay XP at about 77% of roaming's rate — enough that a dedicated crafter is a real playstyle, especially when market demand for crafted goods is high.

The catalog holds **49 recipes** across gear, consumables, fuel cells, and patch kits, plus 3 batch recipes gated behind [[Actions and Occupations|Research]], and gear crafting is capped at **Epic** — Legendaries are found, not made. Crafted consumables carry a **quality percentage** that the game actually reads; the NPC [[Vendor]] sells only flat-100% baseline staples, so quality above baseline — and whole consumable types the vendor never stocks — exist only because a crafter made them.
