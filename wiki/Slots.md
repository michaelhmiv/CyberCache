# Slots

The slot machine is the game's declared gambling lane, and its math is published, not vibes: the paytable is data, and its credit return-to-player is **exactly 50.5%** — the machine is a credit sink with prizes, not a faucet. Bet tiers are 50 / 250 / 1,000 credits, capped at **100 pulls per day**.

Prizes are credits and a staples bundle worth roughly double the bet. The rare prize (0.15% of pulls) is a **cache key**, charged against your daily [[Caches|cache-box budget]] like any other opening source — if your budget is full, it pays 5 fragments instead. The machine never pays gear, cores, scrap, or essences. Because it's real-economy transacting, pulling requires a signed-in (verified) account — guests can see the machine and its printed odds.
