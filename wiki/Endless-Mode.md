# Endless Mode

Endless mode is the post-campaign proving ground: an unbounded descent where each depth scales the opposition — HP compounds by about 12% per depth and damage by about 10% — so every build eventually meets its wall.

Death in endless is **contained**: it ends the run without the death-and-resurrection consequences of the open world, and your banked payout is taxed rather than wiped — by default you keep half of the run's earnings (the endless death payout fraction). How deep a full Epic build at your level should expect to reach is a tuning question the design treats as an open observation, not a promise.
