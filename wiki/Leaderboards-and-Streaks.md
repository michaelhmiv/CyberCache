# Leaderboards & Streaks

**Weekly leaderboards** run 8 race definitions, of which 4 run each week on a seeded rotation — fastest progress, richest hauls, deepest descents, and more. Settlement rides the daily server heartbeat; podium finishers earn cache keys (3 / 2 / 1) and drop-luck buffs for the following week.

The **daily login streak** rewards showing up: a 7-day ladder starting at 100 credits on day 1 and building to 500 credits + 3 key fragments + 1 cache key on day 7, with a tier multiplier (×1.5 per tier, capped at tier 5) as your streak history grows — the key never scales. Both systems surface right on [[The Board]]: the streak flame and standings are glanceable without opening a menu.
