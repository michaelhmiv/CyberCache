# Vendor

The NPC vendor is the beginner on-ramp: a counter that sells **baseline generic consumables** — 7 staples at flat 100% quality, no rarities — so a new hunter can always stock up, while anything better (quality above 100%, crafter-only types) comes only from [[Crafting|crafters]] and [[The Market]].

The counter runs a date-seeded rotation with **infinite stock**, a daily **Item of the Day at 25% off**, and a **buyback** desk that pays 40% of an item's value for gear you bring in. Guests can buy and sell at the vendor freely — it's the one storefront whose transacting never required an account.
