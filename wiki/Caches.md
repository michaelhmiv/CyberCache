# Caches

Data Caches are the game's box-opening lane, and they run on **one shared daily budget of openings** — 10 per day by default. The first opening each day is **free**; openings after that cost 1 cache key or one rewarded ad, your choice at the box. Every source of keys and openings draws on the same budget: streak keys, leaderboard keys, contract keys, even a slot-machine key prize all spend from it.

Cache keys are assembled from key fragments earned in play. What a cache forges follows the cache lane's own rules — notably, cache-forged weapons never carry a [[Legendary Effects|legendary effect]], which keeps found legendaries special. Caches are the monetization lane; loot drops are never sold.
