# Progression

CyberCache is a **long-term game**: the XP curve totals **12,480,235 XP to level 100**, modeled at roughly two years of consistent regular play — level 25 arrives in about 2–3 weeks, level 45 in about 6–8 weeks. Skill points are deliberately scarce (your budget is your level − 1 across four trees — Gunslinger, Demolition, Scavenger, Fixer), so builds are commitments; a respec costs 100 credits per spent point.

Offline earnings accrue while you're away up to a level-earned cap (2 hours at low levels growing to 12), plus Scavenger bonuses — but the **absolute ceiling is 24 hours**, and you can set your own limiter lower. On top of levels sit two more ladders: **Cache Rank**, whose tokens come from levels only (174 across a full climb) and are spent across 7 tracks with no respec, and **mastery**, the per-profession XP that measures time spent in each occupation and research course.
