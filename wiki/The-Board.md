# The Board

The Board is your home screen and the first thing you see. Across the top sit your resources — credits, scrap, keys, cores, equipped-loot count, and level — followed by the level bar: your level badge and XP progress, the daily-streak flame, and the contracts icon, each opening its own panel.

Below that: your current **area** (tap to change where roaming happens), the action stage showing your hunter and current activity, a hunter-totals strip, your consumable slots, and the **Action Board** listing what your hunter is doing. Your while-you-were-away recap lives inside the level panel — and if you were gone less than five minutes it never pops up on its own; the settlement still lands and stays tucked there for you to read.
