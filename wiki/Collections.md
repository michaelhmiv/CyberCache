# Collections

Collections are the long-game checklist: **81 entries** tracking the gear, weapons, and finds worth hunting across every system. Completing entries is how a hunter's profile becomes a trophy shelf.

The legendary end of the system runs on **legendary cores**: a core is minted in exactly one way — salvaging a Legendary item in the recycle flow — and cores are the currency of the deepest crafting, including the Legendary Core Catalyst and legendary [[Gear|reforge]] rerolls. Nothing else mints them: not standing turn-ins, not the market, not caches. That scarcity is deliberate — a legendary should always be in demand.
