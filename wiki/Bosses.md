# Bosses

There are **13 bosses**, tiered from the Dustwaste Frontier's first target up to level-75 endgame fights, each with its own sprite, arena, and mechanics. Boss prep shows you the boss — its effects, mechanics, and stats — as intel. It deliberately never shows a computed win chance or readiness score: reading the boss and judging your build is the game.

Attempts cost an entry fee; winning earns XP at a heavy multiplier and a shot at a dedicated boss drop rolled at the higher of the boss's level or yours. **Rematch fees escalate**: each victory over the same boss on the same day doubles the next fee, up to 8× the base. Death is recorded server-side, and resurrection costs scale with the area — its unlock level × 100 credits, capped at 50,000.
