# Repair Board

There is **no NPC mechanic**. Vehicle repair is an open market: anyone posts a job, any mechanic takes it, and the board (a tab inside [[The Market]]) is the only way to hire one — no private hires. **Patch kits** are the consumable for both lanes: the mechanic supplies kits on board jobs, so a job's price is kits plus labor.

Jobs escrow the full price up front and refund pro-rata on unfinished work. Mechanics earn **XP at 2 points per condition point restored**, and their edge is time and efficiency, not permission — you can always self-repair with your own kits, but untrained work is slow and restores less.
