# CyberCache Wiki

Welcome to the CyberCache wiki — the organized reference for everything in the game.

CyberCache is a mobile-first **idle roguelite looter RPG** with Borderlands-inspired loot: weapons rolled across 6 types, 6 manufacturers, 6 elements, and 5 rarities, with affixes and 26 legendary effects on top. The loop: pick an action and let your hunter work — roaming areas for loot and credits, fighting bosses for dedicated drops, crafting gear and consumables as an occupation, trading on a player market — then gear up, push deeper areas and harder bosses, and build toward the endgame of endless depths, collections, and weekly races.

Everything is server-authoritative: the Chef (the game server) resolves every tick, drop, and trade, so what the wiki describes is what the server actually does.
