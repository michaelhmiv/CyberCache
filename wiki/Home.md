# CyberCache Wiki

Welcome to the CyberCache wiki — the organized reference for everything in the game, written for players.

CyberCache is a mobile-first **idle roguelite looter RPG** with Borderlands-inspired loot. Your hunter works while you watch — and keeps working, within limits, while you're away. You make the decisions: where to roam, what to equip, which boss to prep for, what to craft, and what to sell.

## The loot

Weapons roll across **6 weapon types** (Pistol, SMG, Shotgun, Sniper, Launcher, Beam), **6 manufacturers**, **6 elements**, and **5 rarities** — Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, and Legendary — with rolled affixes on top. Legendary weapons can carry one of **25 named legendary effects**, the signature behaviors that endgame builds are built around. See [[Gear]] and [[Legendary Effects]] for how a drop is put together.

## The core loop

1. **Pick an action.** Your hunter does one thing at a time — roaming an area for loot and credits, fighting a boss, crafting, extracting, and more. See [[Actions and Occupations]].
2. **Loot drops.** Roaming ticks, boss kills, caches, and endless runs all feed your inventory. Equip the upgrades, salvage or sell the rest.
3. **Gear up and push deeper.** Stronger gear opens harder areas and higher boss tiers, which drop stronger gear.
4. **Build the long game.** Levels, skill trees, [[Progression|Cache Rank]], [[Collections]], and [[Leaderboards and Streaks|weekly races]] reward months of play, not a single weekend.

Everything is server-authoritative: the game server resolves every tick, drop, and trade, so what this wiki describes is what the server actually does — never what a screen merely displays.
