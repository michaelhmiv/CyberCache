# Getting Started

You can start playing instantly as a **guest** — no account, no sign-in wall. When you're ready, sign in (Google on Android, Apple on iOS) and your guest progress carries over to the account; you keep the same hunter, gear, and credits.

Every new hunter receives a **starter bundle**: 5,000 credits and a Scrap Bike (your first vehicle), granted automatically the first time your profile loads. Spend your first session roaming the Dustwaste Frontier to learn the tick rhythm, equip whatever drops, crack your free daily cache, and take your first boss prep when a weapon feels strong enough.

Signing in unlocks transacting: buying, selling, and listing on [[The Market]], posting and accepting jobs on the [[Repair Board]] and [[Shield-Tech]], and pulling [[Slots]]. Guests can see everything — the market, the boards, the leaderboards — with real data; only transacting requires an account. Crafting never requires one.
